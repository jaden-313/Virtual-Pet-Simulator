Ruby Pet Simulator

Files:
- main.rb: Starts the game, handles adoption and the main menu.
- pet.rb: Defines the Pet class, animal subclasses, statistics, money, and shop purchases.
- activities.rb: Defines feeding, playing, and sleeping.
- pet_needs.rb: Updates pet needs over elapsed time and adjusts health.

Requirements:
- Ruby installed on your computer.

How to run:
1. Extract the PetSimulator ZIP file.
2. Open a terminal in the PetSimulator folder.
3. Run: ruby main.rb

Starting money: $100

Activity rewards:
- Feed: $2
- Play: $5
- Sleep: $2

Pet shop:
- Premium Food: $15
- Toy: $20
- Energy Snack: $15
- Medicine: $30
- Treat: $5

Note:
Pet needs update when the player performs an activity or checks statistics.
Game progress is not saved after exiting.
