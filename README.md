# Virtual Pet Simulator

## Description

The Virtual Pet Simulator is a Ruby application that allows users to choose between a cat, dog, rabbit, or horse and give their pet a name. Users can take care of their pet by feeding it, playing with it, and letting it sleep.

Each pet has its own hunger, happiness, energy, and health levels that change over time and depending on the user's actions. Mood symbols indicate whether the pet is happy, sad, excited, hungry, or tired.

Different pet types will have their own behaviors and needs. Users can also save their progress and continue where they left off.

## Features

- Choose between a cat, dog, rabbit, or horse
- Name and take care of a virtual pet
- Feed, play with, and let the pet sleep
- Track hunger, happiness, energy, and health levels
- Display mood symbols based on the pet's condition
- Different behaviors and needs for each pet type
- Save and load progress
